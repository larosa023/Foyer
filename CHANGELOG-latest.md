## Foyer 0.3.0

### Search that finds everything
- **Files and folders** from your Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Pictures, Music, Videos and OneDrive. If [Everything](https://www.voidtools.com) is running, Foyer uses it and searches every drive instantly; otherwise it keeps its own small index that refreshes at low priority and pauses while you game.
- **Settings and Control Panel**: type "bluetooth", "display", "device manager", "environment variables" and jump straight there.
- **Calculator**: `12*7.5`, `20% of 50`, `sqrt(144)`, `(3+4)*2`. Enter copies the answer.
- **Unit conversions**, including construction units: `5 cy to cf`, `1250 sf to sy`, `12 ft to m`, `72 f in c`, `100 psi to kpa`.
- **Prefixes**: `f:` files only, `s:` settings only, `a:` apps only, `>` run a command (Ctrl+Shift+Enter runs it as administrator).
- Results are grouped (Best match, Apps, Settings, Files & folders) and **things you open often move up** over time.

### Recent
- Recently opened files and folders only, never "recently added" apps. Right-click to pin one or remove it; turn Recent off in Settings.

### Pinning
- **Drag** tiles to reorder them, and drag apps, files and search results onto Pinned to pin them.
- **Folders**: drop one tile onto another to group them. Rename or ungroup any time.
- **Rename** any tile (F2) and give it a **custom icon** (.ico, .png, .exe, .dll).
- **Pin files, folders and websites**, not just apps (right-click the Pinned area).
- **My Apps**: an automatic folder with every larosa023 app on this PC.

### Everything else
- **Bottom bar shortcuts**: choose up to 6 (Downloads, Documents, Task Manager, Device Manager, Terminal, Recycle Bin and more) in Settings, Layout.
- **Instant icons after boot**: icons are cached on disk.
- **Theme files**: export your look as a small JSON file, import and share themes.
- **Font picker** and **text size**.
- **Accessibility**: Narrator reads the selected item and the search box; high contrast, Windows text size and "animation effects off" are respected.
- **Work over admin windows** (optional): Foyer can start as administrator at sign-in so it opens even when an admin app is focused. Apps you launch still run as you, not as administrator.
- **Installer**: Foyer can install itself to your Programs folder, the Start menu and Apps & features, and uninstall cleanly. Portable mode still works.
